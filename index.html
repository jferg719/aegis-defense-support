Aegis Senior Fraud Defense

A mobile defense and evidence capture tool designed to help older adults and caregivers identify scams upstream and protect personal assets.

  • On-Device Verification: Screen suspicious physical mail, utility notices, and payment solicitations.
  • Voice-Guided Interface: High-contrast controls with built-in voice assistance.
  • Intake-Ready Dossiers: Generate structured evidence summaries for family, banking institutions, or Older Adult Protective Services (OAPS).
